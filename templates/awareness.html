🌍 Waste Awareness

📉

Reduce

Buy only what you need, avoid single-use items, carry a cloth bag and choose products with less packaging. The best waste is the waste never created.

🔁

Reuse

Use glass jars as containers, give old clothes and books to others, repair instead of replacing, and use both sides of paper.

♻️

Recycle

Paper, glass, metal and many plastics can be turned into new products. Keep them clean and dry and give them to recycling collectors.

🟢🔵🔴

Waste Segregation

Use separate bins: green for wet, blue for dry and recyclable, red for hazardous. Segregation at home makes recycling and composting possible.

🌱

Composting

Put vegetable peels, fruit waste and dry leaves in a pot or pit. Keep it moist and turn it weekly. In 6–8 weeks you get natural fertiliser.

🚫

Plastic Reduction

Say no to plastic bags and straws, use refillable bottles and steel lunch boxes, and never burn plastic as it releases toxic gases.
